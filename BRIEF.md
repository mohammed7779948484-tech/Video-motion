## Intent
A 15-second vertical motion-graphics ad for SYRAZEN's Web Design & Development services.
Audience (clarified by the user mid-run): non-technical business owners, entrepreneurs and shop
owners who do not yet have a professional website and don't care how websites are designed or
coded. The ad must make them recognise the value of a website and want one: sell the outcome
(being found, open 24/7, customers arriving while you sleep), not the process. Original, premium,
entirely motion-graphics-driven. Chosen concept: "Open All Night" (supersedes "The Render Line").
